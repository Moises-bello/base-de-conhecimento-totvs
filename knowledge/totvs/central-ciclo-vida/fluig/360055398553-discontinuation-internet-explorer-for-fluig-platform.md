In light of Microsoft's disclosure on the Internet Explorer browser life cycle, we announce that support for the fluig platform with Internet Explorer browser will end on 3/01/2021.

As of that date, TOTVS will no longer support the fluig platform with this browser.

We suggest to customers who still use the Internet Explorer browser to adopt one of the browsers approved for the fluig platform, such as Chrome or Firefox.

The TOTVS team is available to answer any questions and perform all necessary procedures to meet your requests, ensuring that all services and products provided are always in accordance with the quality and satisfaction that TOTVS provides to its customers.

[Click here](https://g1.globo.com/economia/tecnologia/noticia/2020/08/20/microsoft-anuncia-aposentadoria-do-internet-explorer.ghtml) and learn more about the discontinuation the Internet Explorer browser.
