With the purpose of increasing the competitive capacity of its customers, TOTVS has been constantly working on the functional and technological evolution of its products and, for this reason, we communicate that the **Datasul JBoss** functionality will be discontinued on **5/31/2022.**

In accordance with the TOTVS Software Lifecycle Policy, as of the date of discontinuation, published on the Customer Portal, TOTVS will not evolve, support or maintain the discontinued functionality.

We suggest that customers who still use Datasul JBoss 4.2.3 GA, adopt the **Datasul for THF** solution (DTS4THF), which offers simpler architecture to be managed, secure, with better performance and scalable, due to the technological evolution of components, already consolidated by the market.

Find out how the transition to adopting the new **Datasul for THF** functionality will be.

Until 5/31/2021 - Planning and adoption of Datasul for THF (DTS4THF).

**Pre-requirement:** Software updated to version 12.1.29 or higher. Version 12.1.29 is expected to be released on 9/4/2020.

From 6/1/2021 to 5/31/2022 - JBoss will not be updated with new features. Only technical support and error fixing will be maintained.

As of 5/31/2022 - JBoss will be discontinued and there will be no further expeditions, technical support or error fixing.

![mceclip0.png](https://centraldeatendimento.totvs.com/hc/article_attachments/360091791993/mceclip0.png)

To ensure the successful adoption of the new solution, it is essential to carry out the validation of the product's adherence, definition of specific configurations and new parameterizations, followed by rounds of tests. It is worth mentioning that the implementation of the chosen solution may generate the need for training and qualifications, which can be provided on demand.

The TOTVS team is available to answer any remaining doubts and carry out all the necessary procedures to attend to your requests, ensuring that all services and products provided are always in accordance with the quality and satisfaction that TOTVS provides to its customers.
