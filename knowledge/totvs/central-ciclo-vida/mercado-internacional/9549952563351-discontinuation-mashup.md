Dear Customer,

 TOTVS products are in constant evolution to overcome challenges and follow each step of your company's digital journey.

We are working to adequate our products to the market's strategies and needs.

Because of that, we inform you that the **Mashup** solution will be discontinued on **April 15, 2023**

Below, we clarify possible questions related to the discontinuation of the **Mashup** solution:

**Will I be able to access Mashup after the discontinuation date?**

No. The Mashup will no longer be accessible after the discontinuation date.

**What will happen after the discontinuation date?**

Following the TOTVS Software Life Cycle Policy, as of the date of discontinuation, TOTVS will not evolve, support, or maintain the discontinued solution.

We are available to solve any questions. Contact your ESN (Business Solutions Manager)!
