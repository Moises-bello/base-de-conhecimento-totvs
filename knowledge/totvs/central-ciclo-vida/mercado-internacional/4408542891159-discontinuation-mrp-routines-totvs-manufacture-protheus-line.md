With the mission of enhancing the competitive capacity of its customers, TOTVS has been constantly working on the functional and technological evolution of its products; therefore, we inform that some routines from the Production Planning and Control module of the **MRP - Material Requirement Planning** will be discontinued on **August 31, 2022**.

Following the TOTVS Software Lifecycle Policy, as of the date of discontinuation published on the Customer Portal, TOTVS will not evolve, support or maintain the discontinued routines.

We suggest that customers who still use the old MRP routines start using the **new routines**, which were developed with the same concept as the ones that will be discontinued, but with a focus on improving the experience of our customers. 

Learn about the routines that will be discontinued, the replacement routines, and their benefits: 

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Discontinued Routine** | **New Routine** | **Characteristics of the new routine** |
| MATA200 - Framework | PCPA200 - Framework | Simpler usability and better performance when the framework has multiple levels. |
| MATA202 - Pre-framework | PCPA135 - Pre-framework | Simpler usability and better performance when the framework has multiple levels. |
| MATA 632 - Productive Process | PCPA124 - Productive Process, or   PCPA129 - Productive Process by Framework | Simpler usability when performing the productive process considering the product framework. |
| MATA380 - Simple Allocation | MATA381 - Multiple Allocation | Better usability. |
| MATA712 - MRP | PCPA712 - MRP | Superior performance and new features, such as demand control. |
| PCPA107 - Multi-company MRP | PCPA712 - MRP | The new MRP routine already includes the multi-company resource. That way, you can use PCPA712 when performing the calculation for a single company or the multi-company concept.  Its calculation performance is also superior. |
| MATR882 - MRP Report | PCPA712 - Report | Report for analyzing the calculation result. The new PCPA712 routine has a print option that allows exporting to Excel. |

The new routines are already available and can be accessed using the MRP (Production Planning and Control module).

As of the discontinuation date, the old routines will no longer be available for access, but their data can be accessed using the new routines.

More details are available in the Technical Documentation on our TDN page:

<https://tdn.totvs.com/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=286008584>.

To ensure the successful adoption of the new solution, it is essential to carry out the validation of the product's adherence, the definition of specific configurations, and new parameterizations, followed by rounds of tests. It is worth mentioning that the implementation of the chosen solution may generate the need for training and qualifications, which can be provided on demand.

We are at your disposal to clear up any questions and carry out all the necessary procedures for your requests. Our goal is to ensure that all our services and products are always up to the quality and satisfaction that we work to provide our customers.

Should you have any questions, contact your Solution and Business Executive.
